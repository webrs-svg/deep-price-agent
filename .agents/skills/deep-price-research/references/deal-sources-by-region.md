# High-Signal Price & Deal Sources by Region (`deal-sources-by-region.md`)

Subagents should prioritize these high-signal price comparison engines, deal communities, and cashback aggregators based on the target region.

---

## 1. Australia (`AU` / `AUD` — Default)

### Price Comparison & Search Aggregators
- **StaticICE (`staticice.com.au`)**: Essential for tech, PC parts, cameras, networking, and electronics across independent AU stores. Specify `state:nsw` / `state:vic` etc. when filtering.
- **CamelCamelCamel AU (`au.camelcamelcamel.com`)**: Amazon Australia historical price tracking (both Amazon AU local and Amazon Global Store US/UK/JP imports).
- **PriceMe (`priceme.com.au`) & GetPrice (`getprice.com.au`)**: Broad appliance and consumer goods comparison.
- **Google Shopping AU (`google.com.au/shopping`)**: Live feed across big-box and boutique Shopify stores.

### Community Deal Forums (Ground Truth for Real Street Prices)
- **OzBargain (`ozbargain.com.au`)**: The #1 source in Australia for historical low prices, stacking tricks, discounted gift card promos, and active coupon codes. Always search `site:ozbargain.com.au "<product name>"`.
- **Whirlpool Forums (`forums.whirlpool.net.au`)**: "Good Guys / JB Hi-Fi / Harvey Norman what price did you pay?" threads for TVs, whitegoods, and appliances.

### Cashback & Gift Card Stacking (AU)
- **ShopBack AU (`shopback.com.au`)** & **TopCashback AU (`topcashback.com.au`)**: Compare rates before checkout (often 5%–20% upsized on electronics/fashion/travel).
- **Discounted Gift Cards**:
  - Coles / Woolworths weekly catalogue promotions (often 10% off or 20x Flybuys/Everyday Rewards points ≈ 10% back on Ultimate, TCN, JB Hi-Fi, Apple, or Uber gift cards).
  - Member portals (Macquarie Marketplace, NRMA, RACV, RACQ, AIA Vitality, Union Shopper, Suncorp) offering 4%–8% instant discount on JB Hi-Fi, The Good Guys, Officeworks, Amazon AU, and Woolworths eGift cards.

### Retailer Price-Match / Price-Beat Policies (AU)
- **Officeworks**: **5% Price Beat Guarantee** on identical in-stock items (including Amazon AU shipped-and-sold-by-Amazon, JB Hi-Fi, etc.).
- **JB Hi-Fi**: Price match via Live Chat, phone, or "Ask for a Deal" button on product pages (must be an authorized AU retailer with live stock, excludes grey imports).
- **The Good Guys**: Price Match + StoreCash promotions.
- **Bunnings**: 10% Price Guarantee on identical stocked hardware/smart-home items.
- **Appliances Online**: Free next-day delivery + removal of old appliance, readily price-matches authorized retailers via phone/chat.

## 2. United States (`US` / `USD`)

### Price Comparison & History
- **CamelCamelCamel (`camelcamelcamel.com`) & Keepa (`keepa.com`)**: Amazon US price history.
- **PCPartPicker (`pcpartpicker.com`)**: Live pricing + promo codes across Newegg, Amazon, B&H, Best Buy, Adorama, Micro Center.
- **Google Shopping US**: Broad merchant coverage.

### Deal Communities & Cashback
- **Slickdeals (`slickdeals.net`)**: Search `site:slickdeals.net "<product name>"` for frontpage deal benchmarks and promo stacks.
- **Reddit**: `r/buildapcsales`, `r/deals`, `r/frugalmalefashion`, `r/GameDeals`, `r/HomeLabSales`.
- **CashbackMonitor (`cashbackmonitor.com`)**: Compares Rakuten, TopCashback US, RetailMeNot, Capital One Shopping, and airline mileage portals in one table.
- **Price Match Policies**: Best Buy Price Match Guarantee (matches Amazon, B&H, Crutchfield, Costco, Target, Walmart), Micro Center, Target, Home Depot.

## 3. United Kingdom & Europe (`UK` / `GBP` & `EU` / `EUR`)

- **Deal Communities**: **HotUKDeals (`hotukdeals.com`)**, **mydealz (`mydealz.de`)**, **Dealabs (`dealabs.com`)**, **Chollometro (`chollometro.com`)**.
- **Price Aggregators**: **Idealo (`idealo.co.uk` / `idealo.de`)**, **Geizhals (`geizhals.eu` / `skinny.co.uk`)**, **PriceSpy (`pricespy.co.uk`)**, **Currys Price Promise**, **John Lewis Never Knowingly Undersold**.
- **Cashback**: TopCashback UK, Quidco.

---

## 4. Global Travel, Flights & Accommodations

- **Flight Aggregators**: Google Flights, Skyscanner, ITA Matrix, BeatThatFlight (AU), Secret Flying, Momondo.
- **Hotel & OTA Comparison**: Google Hotels (compares direct vs Booking.com, Agoda, Trip.com, Expedia, Hotels.com), Trivago.
- **Stacking Strategies**:
  - Check **ShopBack / TopCashback / Rakuten** for Booking.com (up to 10–14% upsized), Agoda, Expedia, Trip.com, and Qatar/Etihad/Emirates.
  - Check **Direct Hotel Best Rate Guarantee (BRG)**: Marriott (25% off lower OTA rate or 5,000 points), Hilton (25% off lower OTA rate), Hyatt (20% off or 5,000 points), Accor (10%–25% off).
  - Compare mobile-only rates vs desktop rates on Booking.com (Genius Level 1–3) and Agoda.
